This page documents the testing of the restricted product visibility feature for AllSurfaces OroCommerce.

## Overview

The restricted product visibility feature allows products to be hidden from search results based on their P21 sellable status. When the feature is enabled, products with `restricted_visibility = true` and `p21_sellable = false` are hidden from search results.

## Test Results

### Product Visible (Feature OFF)

The product QEP82509BR is visible when the feature is OFF.

![Product visible](/screenshots/screenshot-product-visible.png)

### Product Hidden (Feature ON)

The product QEP82509BR is hidden when the feature is ON.

![Product hidden](/screenshots/screenshot-product-hidden.png)

## Conclusion

The restricted product visibility feature is working as expected. The product QEP82509BR is visible when the feature is OFF and hidden when the feature is ON.
